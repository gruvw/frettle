            About

            Frettle is a free, open source app that shows every note, chord shape and scale on the neck of a guitar, bass, ukulele, mandolin, mandola or banjo.

            Pick a chord to see all the ways to play it up the neck, with the root and intervals marked and barres drawn in. Pick a scale to see it across the whole fretboard, split into hand-sized groups, along with the chords that fit it. Tap any note to hear it.

            It covers 6 and 7-string guitar, 4 and 5-string bass, ukulele (high or low G), baritone ukulele, mandolin, mandola and 5-string banjo, in standard tuning or Drop D, DADGAD, open G, double C and other alternate tunings, with or without a capo. It has no ads and works offline.
