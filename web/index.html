        Frettle is a free fretboard visualizer for guitar, bass, ukulele (high or low G) and baritone ukulele. See every note on the neck, find chord shapes with their intervals in any key, highlight major, minor, pentatonic and modal scales with their triads, place a capo, map the octaves and click any note to hear it. Made for players learning the fretboard, especially those coming from the piano.
